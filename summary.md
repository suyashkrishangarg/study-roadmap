# Study Platform — Feature Summary

A shared study dashboard for a small friend group. One Google sign-in, one
shared workspace, everything visible to the group. Deployed at
`study-roadmap-one.vercel.app` (custom domain: `study.ramyaai.tech`).

---

## Getting started

1. Go to the site and **Sign in with Google**.
   - The first person to sign in becomes the **owner** and the workspace is
     created automatically.
   - Everyone else signs in, then enters the **8-character invite code**
     (shown on the dashboard, under "Invite code") to join the workspace.
2. The top nav has: Dashboard, Tasks, Roadmaps, Timeline, Calendar,
   Check-ins, Practice, Quizzes, Flashcards, Marathons, Analytics, Assistant.
3. The **bell icon** (top right) shows notifications: deadlines approaching,
   marathons starting, graded quizzes. Click "Mark all read" to clear.

### Roles

- **Owner / Admin** — can delete roadmaps and marathons, regenerate the invite
  code.
- **Member** — can create and edit their own content; can edit/delete anything
  they authored. Only the author or an admin can edit/delete an item.

---

## Dashboard (`/`)

Your daily home screen:

- **Stat cards** — minutes studied today, current streak (consecutive days with
  any check-in), number of active goals.
- **Last 7 days chart** — minutes studied per day (bar chart).
- **Task completion chart** — tasks completed per day over the last 14 days.
- **Due soon** — tasks assigned to you or created by you that are due today or
  tomorrow, with deadline badges. You can mark tasks done directly from here.
- **Log study time** — quick check-in form (subject + duration + optional
  note). This feeds your streak, heatmap, and goals.
- **Goals** — your daily/weekly minute targets with progress bars; create,
  edit, and delete goals here.
- **Invite code card** — copy the code to share, or regenerate it (admin).

---

## Tasks (`/tasks`)

- **New task** — title, notes, priority (low/medium/high), due date, assignee.
- **Filters** — by due window (all/today/this week/overdue), status
  (to do/in progress/done), priority, and assignee (everyone/me). Filters are
  saved in the URL so you can bookmark a view.
- Each task row: mark done, edit, delete, deadline badge, priority badge.
- Tasks can optionally be linked to a roadmap item.

---

## Roadmaps (`/roadmaps`)

Long-term plans (a course, exam prep, a project) broken into trackable items.

- **New roadmap** — title, description, start/end date, color.
- Roadmap list shows progress bar, date range, and "x/y done".
- **Roadmap detail** (`/roadmaps/[id]`):
  - Add items with title, description, start/end dates, assignee, and progress
    (0–100).
  - Each item has a status dropdown (to do / in progress / done), edit, and
    delete.
  - The roadmap progress bar updates automatically from item progress.

---

## Timeline (`/timeline`)

A Gantt view of all roadmap items across the workspace:

- Month grid with day columns; weekend shading; a **Today** marker.
- Each roadmap item is a **draggable bar** — drag it to reschedule; the new
  dates are saved automatically.
- Items with no dates are listed separately.

---

## Calendar (`/calendar`)

Month grid showing:

- **Task due dates** and **roadmap item end dates** as chips.
- Click a chip to jump to the task or roadmap.
- Navigate months with the arrows; the month is stored in the URL.

---

## Check-ins (`/check-ins`)

Your study attendance log:

- **Stat cards** — current streak, today's minutes, total minutes (6 months).
- **26-week heatmap** — GitHub-style; a day is "present" if you logged any
  study time that day. Hover a cell for the exact minutes.
- **History** — grouped list of every check-in (date, subject, duration, note).
- **Check-in form** — subject, duration (minutes), optional note. One check-in
  per subject per day (adding more time to the same subject the same day adds
  to it).

---

## Practice (`/practice`)

A question bank for self-testing:

- **New question** — topic, difficulty (easy/medium/hard), prompt, options
  (one per line, for MCQ-style), solution.
- **Filters** — by topic, difficulty, and mastered status.
- **Mark mastered** — flag questions you've nailed; track what you've mastered
  per topic.
- Questions can be created manually or generated by AI (see AI section).

---

## Quizzes (`/quizzes`)

- **Create a quiz** three ways:
  - **Manual** — add questions one by one (MCQ, true/false, or short answer;
    MCQ/true-false auto-grade, short answer is self-checked).
  - **Template** — starter question sets.
  - **AI** — give a topic (and optional notes) and Gemini generates a full
    quiz with questions, options, answers, and explanations.
- **Take a quiz** (`/quizzes/[id]/take`) — one question at a time, then
  auto-graded: score, per-question review, correct answers and explanations.
- **Quiz detail** (`/quizzes/[id]`) — your attempt history with scores and
  dates, per-topic accuracy.

---

## Flashcards (`/flashcards`)

Spaced-repetition flashcards (SM-2 scheduler):

- **Review tab (default)** — shows cards due today. Flip the card, then grade
  yourself: **Again / Hard / Good / Easy**. The scheduler sets the next review
  date based on your grade (Again = 1 day, then 1 → 6 → interval × ease).
  Grading "Again" re-queues the card in the same session.
- **Browse all tab** — all your cards, filterable by topic; flip to study;
  delete any card.
- **New flashcard** — front (prompt), back (answer), optional topic.
- **AI generation** — enter a topic and Gemini generates a set of cards.
- Stats row: due count, learning, mastered, and overall mastery %.

---

## Marathons (`/marathons`)

Focused group study events:

- **Two types:**
  - **Scheduled** — fixed start time; shows a live countdown until it starts.
  - **On-demand** — start anytime.
- **Start a session** — a live timer runs (mm:ss), with a progress bar toward
  the goal minutes. Minutes are logged live to the leaderboard.
- **End session** — your minutes are saved and also count as a study
  check-in, so marathon time counts toward your **goals and streaks**.
- **Leaderboard** — per-member minutes, sorted; completion rate (finished
  sessions ÷ participants).
- Admin/owner can delete a marathon (and its sessions).

---

## Analytics (`/analytics`)

- **Stat cards** — day streak, minutes this week, card retention %, cards due.
- **Subject mastery radar** — quiz accuracy vs. practice-question mastery per
  topic (ApexCharts).
- **Retention curve** — average flashcard interval by repetition count (how
  your SM-2 scheduling is working).
- **Activity polygraph** — last 14 days on three axes: study minutes, tasks
  completed, and quiz score %.
- **Leaderboard** — the whole group: this week's minutes, streak, tasks done,
  quiz average, marathon minutes.
- **Badges** — 11 earnable badges (First Steps, On a Roll, Week Warrior,
  Marathoner, Scholar, Quiz Debut, Perfect Score, Card Collector, Marathon
  Finisher, Task Crusher, Early Bird).

---

## AI Assistant (`/assistant`)

A streaming chat with your workspace. It can **see and edit** your data via
tools — you don't just get advice, it does the work:

- **Reads**: study summary (today's minutes, streak, due tasks), tasks,
  roadmaps, check-ins, goals, practice questions, quizzes.
- **Writes**: create/update/delete tasks, roadmaps, roadmap items, check-ins
  (log study time), goals, practice questions.
- Example prompts:
  - "What should I focus on today?"
  - "Add a task: study maths for 2 hours, due 10 October"
  - "Log 45 minutes of biology"
  - "What tasks are due this week?"
  - "Create a weekly goal of 300 minutes"
- Tool calls are shown inline as collapsible cards (what it did, with the
  result), so you can see and verify every action.
- Powered by **Gemini Flash Lite Latest** (`gemini-flash-lite-latest`), streaming
  via the Vercel AI SDK. Chat history is saved per user.

### AI generation (quizzes & flashcards)

- Quiz generation and flashcard generation both run server-side with the same
  Gemini model; the API key never reaches the browser.
- **Daily caps** (per user, to keep the free tier sustainable): 5 AI quizzes
  and 10 AI flashcard sets per day. You'll see a clear message when you hit
  the cap.

## Tips to get the most out of it

- **Check in daily** — even 1 minute keeps your streak alive and fills the
  heatmap.
- **Set a daily goal** on the dashboard (e.g. 120 min) — the progress ring
  updates live as you check in.
- **Review flashcards every day** — the SM-2 queue is most effective with
  daily reviews; "Again" cards come back in the same session.
- **Run a marathon** before big exams — on-demand timers double as focused
  sessions and count toward goals.
- **Let the assistant do bookkeeping** — "log 30 minutes of chemistry" or
  "add a task … due Friday" is faster than the forms.
- **Analytics radar** — compare quiz accuracy vs. practice mastery per topic to
  decide what to study next.

## Technical notes

- Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, shadcn/ui.
- Auth: Auth.js v5 (Google OAuth). Session re-reads your role/workspace from
  the DB on every request.
- Database: Neon serverless Postgres + Prisma ORM.
- Charts: Recharts (dashboard), ApexCharts (analytics radar/polygraph/retention).
- AI: Vercel AI SDK v7 + `@ai-sdk/google`, model `gemini-flash-lite-latest`.
- All mutations are workspace-scoped server actions with authorization checks
  (`requireMember` / `requireAdmin` / `canEdit`).
